# Sudoku for Dads

[Choose a language](https://github.com/robertpbyrne/SudokuForDad/wiki)

## Player guide

No ads. No pressure. Just Sudoku.

Made for my dad, Wayne, and for anyone who enjoys a good puzzle. Sudoku for Dads gives you a quiet place to think, with a little humour waiting at the finish.

• Play fresh daily puzzles.
• Choose from four difficulty levels, from relaxed to demanding.
• Enter answers with the pen pad and possibilities with the pencil pad.
• Use hints when you want a hand, or play in Pure mode without helpers.
• Follow your progress with stats and daily streaks.
• Finish a puzzle, enjoy the celebration and discover a dad joke.

Take a little time for yourself. One square at a time.

## How to play

Fill each row, column and 3×3 box with the digits 1–9, without repeats. Select a cell, then use the pen pad to enter an answer or the pencil pad to mark possibilities. In Assisted mode, hints are available when you need help.
